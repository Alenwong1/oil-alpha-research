# Inventory and trading refinement — full-period development

Frozen before scoring this batch. Evaluate 2017–2025 without removing years; leave 2026 closed. Retain the current 0.942-Sharpe blend as benchmark.

Inventory forecasts: predict each week's crude, gasoline and distillate stock change from two annual seasonal harmonics, the previous released stock change and its trailing four-release mean, and previously released imports, exports and refinery inputs. Ridge alpha 10 with training-only scaling, at least 104 prior complete observations, at most five years of history. Each weekly forecast is made using strictly earlier releases. Surprise is actual change minus that forecast, normalized by the standard deviation of at least 26 earlier out-of-sample forecast errors (maximum 104). These are model surprises, not analyst consensus. Compare weekly forecast RMSE with the existing historical seasonal-mean expectation.

Trading models: retain the existing inventory model's saved forecasts, and fit one replacement using the three new surprise features plus the four existing flow/usage seasonal features. Same quarterly expanding ridge alpha 100, 21-session normalized excess-return target, purged labels, and 252 complete training observations. Missing features disable a sleeve.

Exactly 24 policies: two inventory forecast models × three minimum-strength hurdles (0, 0.25, 0.50 times past normalized forecast-error RMS) × two risk rules (existing 252-session sleeve volatility; conservative max of 63-/252-session volatility plus uncertainty haircut), each tested standalone and as a fixed 50/50 exposure blend with the saved prior long/cash model. Do not optimize blend weights. Only completed 21-session predictions may contribute to error RMS (at least 126, trailing 252 observations). Error RMS measures historical predictive error, not a calibrated probability or confidence interval. Haircut is abs(score)/(abs(score)+error RMS). All hurdle variants retain the existing trading-cost gate and 0.10-equity adjustment deadband.

Risk sizing: 15% standalone volatility target, 5% volatility floor, 2x target cap, at least 126 completed daily return observations. Weekly inventory rebalances preserve shares; blended portfolio nets exposures and rebalances daily. Daily risk returns are shifted by two signal rows; label completion dates govern forecast-error statistics. No full-sample parameter fitting. Thresholds are predefined hypotheses, not claimed to be historically selected out of sample.

Primary costs: 5 bps turnover, 3% annual borrow, cash proxy plus 2% financing spread, no short collateral rebate. Report all policies, each year's excess Sharpe, drawdown and return, coverage, trade intensity and cost sensitivity. Retain full-period losses and earlier attempts. Selection among these policies is development selection, not independent validation.
